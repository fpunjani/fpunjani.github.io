Projects

Settlement information can be scattered across court notices, law-firm websites and claim administrators. I built SettleWorth to help Canadians find relevant claims, understand the requirements and keep track of what to do next.

People can explore settlements, check what may apply to them, save opportunities and follow links to the official claim process.
